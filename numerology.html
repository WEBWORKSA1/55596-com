Numerology

Numerology reduces dates and names to single digits and reads each digit as a personality theme. It's symbolic, not scientific — and a lot of fun when you know how the calculation works.

The core numbers

  • Life path — from your full date of birth. Calculate
  • Expression (destiny) — from every letter of your full birth name. Calculate
  • Soul urge — vowels only; your inner motivation.
  • Personality — consonants only; how others see you.
  • Personal year — your birth month and day plus the current year. Calculate

How a life path is calculated

Take a birthday of 23 August 1990. Reduce each part separately: month 8 → 8; day 23 → 2+3 = 5; year 1990 → 1+9+9+0 = 19 → 1+9 = 10 → 1. Then add: 8 + 5 + 1 = 14 → 1+4 = 5. Life path 5 — change and freedom. Reducing each part first is the method most numerologists use, because it preserves master numbers that a single long sum can hide.

Questions

Pythagorean or Chaldean — which is right?

They're different traditions. Pythagorean assigns 1–9 in alphabet order and is most common in the West; Chaldean uses 1–8 based on sound and is older. Our name calculator offers both.

Why are 11, 22 and 33 not reduced?

Numerologists treat them as 'master numbers' with an intensified meaning, so they're kept when they appear as a total.

Is numerology scientific?

No. It's a symbolic tradition. People use it for reflection and fun; treat it as you would a horoscope.
