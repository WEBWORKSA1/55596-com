Number decoder

Enter up to 15 digits. You'll get the numerology root, angel-number reading, a digit-by-digit Chinese reading with known combinations, slang matches, a luck score and math facts. Nothing you type leaves your device.

How the decoder works

Numerology root. We add the digits and keep adding until one digit remains, except for the master numbers 11, 22 and 33, which numerologists keep intact.

Angel reading. Repeating numbers (555), mirror pairs (1212) and sequences (1234) get a pattern reading; other numbers are read by their dominant digit.

Culture. Each digit is shown with its Chinese character and the word it sounds like. We also search the number for well-known combinations such as 168, 520, 1314 and 14.

Luck score. A transparent 0–100 score built from digit weights (8, 6 and 9 up; 4 down), the ending digit, unlucky pairs and memorable runs. It reflects tradition and memorability, not destiny.

Math. Prime test, factorisation, divisors, binary, hexadecimal, Roman numerals and the number in words.
