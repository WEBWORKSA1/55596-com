What 911 means

Introduced in the US in 1968, 911 was chosen because it was short, unused as an area code and easy to dial. Europe uses 112, the UK 999, India 112, Australia 000.

In everyday speech '911' also means urgent help, and since 2001 the date 9/11 carries strong historical weight in the US.
