What 9 means

九 (jiǔ) and 久 (jiǔ, long-lasting) are perfect homophones, so 9 is favoured for weddings and anniversaries; 9 roses or 99 roses signify enduring love. Historically 9 was associated with the emperor.

In Japanese, 9 (ku) can sound like 'suffering', so it is less popular there. Numerology treats 9 as completion and compassion.
