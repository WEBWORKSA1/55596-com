What 3 means

三 (sān) is close to 生 (shēng, birth or life), giving it a mildly lucky reputation. Chinese history is full of threes — the Three Kingdoms, three gorges of the Yangtze.

Numerology calls 3 the communicator: playful, social and expressive. It is also the root of 55596, because 5+5+5+9+6 = 30 and 3+0 = 3.
