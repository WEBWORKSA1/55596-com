1314: For a lifetime

1314 sounds like 一生一世 — a whole lifetime — in Mandarin.

Updated October 15, 2026 · Reviewed under our editorial standards

What 1314 means

Paired with 520, it forms 5201314 — 'I love you for a lifetime'. Gift amounts of ¥1314 and wedding registrations on January 3–4 or at 13:14 are popular.

Interestingly, it contains a 4, but the combination's meaning overrides it.

Combinations inside 1314

  • 1314 Chinese: yī sān yī sì ≈ yì shēng yí shì (一生一世) — for a whole lifetime
  • 14 Chinese: yāo sì ≈ yào sǐ (要死) — sounds like 'going to die' — avoided in phone numbers
  • 13 Western: thirteen — traditionally unlucky; many buildings skip floor 13
