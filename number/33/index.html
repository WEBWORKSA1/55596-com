What 33 means

Numerology describes 33 as the 'master teacher', combining the creativity of 3 with service to others. In Chinese, 3 sounds like 'life', so 33 reads as 'life upon life'.

33 also appears in many traditions — the 33 vertebrae of the human spine, and 33 as a symbolic age in several religious stories.
