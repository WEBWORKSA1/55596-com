What 555 means

In Thai, 555 is 'hahaha' because 5 is pronounced 'hâa'. In Chinese chat, the same 555 reads as 呜呜呜 (wū wū wū) — crying. In Japanese gaming, it can mean 'go go go'.

In angel-number tradition, 555 signals big change and freedom. And in North America, 555 is the telephone exchange used for fictional numbers in films and TV; only 555-0100 through 555-0199 are formally reserved for fiction.

555 as internet slang

  • Thai: hahaha — laughter, because 5 is pronounced 'hâa'. More 5s = bigger laugh; '555+' means endless laughing.
  • Chinese (Mandarin): boo-hoo — crying or sulking. The same string reads as laughter in Thai and tears in Chinese.
  • Japanese: go, go, go! — hurry up (gaming). Common in fast-paced game chat.
