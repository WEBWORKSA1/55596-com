What 8 means

八 (bā) sounds like 发 (fā), to prosper. The Beijing Olympics opened at 8:08 pm on 8/8/2008, and Sichuan Airlines reportedly paid about US$280,000 for the phone number 8888-8888 in 2003. Number-plate and phone auctions across China, Hong Kong and Singapore routinely price 8s at a premium.

Numerology agrees in its own way: 8 is ambition, authority and material success. If you are choosing a business number, ending it in 8 is the single simplest 'lucky' upgrade.
