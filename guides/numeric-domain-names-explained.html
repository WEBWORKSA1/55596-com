Numeric domain names explained

Why short number .com domains are valued, how buyers in Asia see them, and what to check before buying one.

By 55596 Editorial · Updated October 15, 2026

Numeric domains — names made only of digits, like 55596.com — are a niche but serious corner of the domain market. They're especially popular with buyers in China and across Asia, where digits are easier to type and remember than Latin-letter brand names.

Why numbers make good domains

  • Universal. Digits read the same in every script; no spelling or translation issues.
  • Memorable when patterned. Repeats (888), sequences (123) and meaningful codes (520, 168) stick.
  • Meaning. In Chinese, a numeric domain can 'say' something: 518 is 'I will prosper', 1314 is 'a lifetime'.
  • Scarcity. Every three-digit .com and four-digit .com has long been registered, and short numeric .com domains trade as a recognisable asset class.

What drives value

  1. Length — shorter is scarcer.
  2. Extension — .com dominates.
  3. Lucky digits (8, 6, 9) and no 4s for Chinese buyers.
  4. Patterns — repeats, mirrors and meaningful codes.
  5. Existing use — traffic and history.

Five-digit domains

Five-digit .coms like 55596 sit in the middle of the market: far more of them exist than four-digit names, so value depends heavily on pattern and meaning. 55596 has a triple-5 run (memorable, and globally meaningful as Thai laughter and an angel number), no 4s, and a 9-6 ending that reads as 'lasting, smooth' in Chinese.

Before you buy

  • Check for trademarks that use the same digits in your field.
  • Check the domain's history for spam or penalties.
  • Use an escrow service for any significant purchase.
