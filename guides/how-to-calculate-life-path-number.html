How to calculate your life path number (with examples)

Step-by-step method, master numbers 11, 22 and 33, and the mistakes most online calculators make.

By 55596 Editorial · Updated October 15, 2026

Your life path number is the starting point of numerology. It comes from your full date of birth and is read as the central theme of your life. The maths is simple, but there's one detail many calculators get wrong.

The method

  1. Reduce the month to a single digit (or master number). October = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1.
  2. Reduce the day. 29 → 2 + 9 = 11. Stop: 11 is a master number.
  3. Reduce the year. 1987 → 1 + 9 + 8 + 7 = 25 → 2 + 5 = 7.
  4. Add the three results and reduce: 1 + 11 + 7 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1.

Life path 1 — independence and leadership.

Why reduce each part first?

If you simply add every digit of the date in one go, you can hide master numbers. Reducing month, day and year separately is the convention used by most numerology authors, and it's what our life path calculator does.

Master numbers

When a total lands on 11, 22 or 33, numerologists keep it rather than reducing to 2, 4 or 6. They're described as intensified versions of their root: 11 the intuitive 'illuminator', 22 the 'master builder', 33 the 'master teacher'.

What each life path means

  • 1 leadership · 2 partnership · 3 expression · 4 structure · 5 freedom
  • 6 care · 7 insight · 8 ambition · 9 compassion
  • 11, 22, 33 master numbers

A healthy way to use it

Numerology is symbolic. Many people find their life path a useful prompt for reflection — 'am I using my strengths?' — rather than a rule. Treat it like a horoscope: interesting, sometimes uncanny, never binding.

Questions

Is the life path the same as a birthday number?

No. The birthday number uses only the day of birth; the life path uses the full date.

What if I get 11?

Keep it — 11 is a master number.
