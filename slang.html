Why do people write words with numbers?

Three reasons come up again and again. Speed: typing 555 is faster than ฮ่าฮ่าฮ่า. Missing letters: early phones had no Arabic keyboard, so 7 stood in for ح. Wordplay: in Chinese and Japanese, numbers sound like syllables, so 520 and 39 became affectionate shortcuts that also slip past filters and look playful. Read our guide to number slang.
